I am an assistant professor in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at the University of South Carolina, interested in understanding the performance behavior of highly-configurable systems. I am especially interested in practical approaches that enable developers and end users to reason about qualities (performance, energy usage, etc). The compelling challenge that drives my research is enabling users to make tradeoff by learning practical models to explore the highly-dimensional configuration space. My research lies in the intersection of software engineering, computer systems, and machine learning.

More information? Please checkout my research, and my public profiles: CV, Google Scholar.
I am always looking for highly-motivated PhD and undergraduate students to work with me at USC. If you are interested, please drop me a line with your areas of interest and your GitHub account.
